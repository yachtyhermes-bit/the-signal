## 1. COMPANY EXPLAINER (for the Writer, plain English)
Rigetti Computing (Berkeley, CA, founded 2013) builds full-stack quantum computers based on **superconducting qubits** — the same chip-fab world as semiconductors — and it's one of the few quantum companies that **manufactures its own processors** (at Fab-1, the industry's first dedicated quantum chip fab) and sells them as **real products people install on-premises**, not just cloud demos. Its product line spans a 9-qubit Novera QPU (plugs into a customer's existing cryo/control gear) up to the 108-qubit Cepheus-1-108Q (twelve 9-qubit chiplets tiled together — the industry's largest multi-chip quantum computer), sold to national labs, governments, universities and research centers, with cloud access via its Quantum Cloud Services (QCS) platform on AWS Braket, Azure and qBraid. Customers include India's C-DAC ($8.4M, 108-qubit system going to Bengaluru), the UK's National Quantum Computing Centre (36-qubit system deployed), HPE + Pittsburgh Supercomputing Center (TangleLab hybrid testbed), plus an Asian tech manufacturer and a California AI startup that bought Novera systems.

## 2. THE FRESH NEWS ANGLE (Aug 19–22, 2026 — verified)

**(a) Systems Delivery Unit — COMPANY NEWS, Aug 19 (GlobeNewswire press release, effective Aug 18):**
- Rigetti established a dedicated **Systems Delivery organization** and created a **new COO role** to run manufacturing operations, systems delivery, commercial functions and customer-facing engineering. Processor architecture/chip development/hardware engineering consolidated under the CTO.
- **David Rivas** (CTO since Feb 2023) → **COO**; **Andrew Bestwick, Ph.D.** (SVP Quantum Systems; led Cepheus chiplet architecture) → **CTO**. Both report to CEO Dr. Subodh Kulkarni.
- Rationale (CEO quote): "growing deployment activity" — on-prem demand now spans 9-qubit Novera to 108-qubit Cepheus-class systems; previously install/support lived inside engineering. New structure = end-to-end accountability for scaling system delivery + engineering focus on the roadmap, incl. a **target of 99.5% median two-qubit gate fidelity on Cepheus-1-108Q** (currently ~99.1%).

## 3. WHY IT MATTERS TO AI (for the Writer)
- **Quantum is the next compute layer after GPUs.** The $2B federal program explicitly funds "hardware optimization" and "data-center-compatible" quantum systems — i.e., quantum as an installed piece of the future data center, the same buildout logic as AI chips.
- **Hybrid quantum-classical is the near-term AI bridge:** HPE + Pittsburgh Supercomputing Center TangleLab (NSF-funded; Rigetti delivering a Novera) is a quantum-classical supercomputer testbed; Rigetti positions QCS as a quantum **co-processor** for classical/AI workloads.
- **Quantum-AI pilots are live:** financial institutions and pharma companies are testing Rigetti's QCS cloud for portfolio optimization and drug discovery (TradingKey) — pilots that become contracts if Lyra (100+ qubit, late 2026) hits fidelity targets.
- **Government treats quantum like semiconductors** — national security tech; the equity-stake structure (Uncle Sam as shareholder) parallels the CHIPS fab buildout and de-risks pre-revenue roadmaps. This is the "AI-adjacent infrastructure buildout" story.
