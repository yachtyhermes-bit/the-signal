## (a) VERIFIED STORY NARRATIVE

1. **Tue Aug 18, 2026** — Robinhood CEO Vlad Tenev publicly urged US regulators (post on X / op-ed) to set a framework for tokenized stocks (blockchain-based equity tokens). Framing: the financial system is in the early stages of a global **"tokenization supercycle"**; America risks watching it happen overseas. Quote: *"It would be a strange outcome if the rest of the world could build the future of ownership around American assets while Americans themselves were left behind."* Also: *"They need to move quickly. Other jurisdictions will not wait for America to catch up."* (CoinDesk, PYMNTS, Benzinga — all Aug 18–19).

2. **Wed Aug 19, 2026** — White House meeting with crypto executives pressing Congress to pass the **Clarity Act** (stalled digital-asset bill, needs 60 Senate votes, final voting window in Sept before recess). Tenev appeared on CNBC's Squawk Box from DC ahead of White House/CFTC meetings: *"We're at the beginning of a supercycle, and it's going to take over the entire financial system."* HOOD gained ~7% intraday on the tokenization push. Robinhood Chain (mainnet Jul 1, 2026) already offers tokenized exposure to **190 US stocks across 120+ countries, backed 1:1, trading 24/7** — but not to Americans.

3. **Thu Aug 20, 2026** — CFTC held its inaugural Innovation Advisory Committee meeting (chair Michael Selig said the agency could build a crypto framework itself if Congress stalls). Bloomberg reported the SEC was set to release a tokenized-stock framework.

4. **Fri Aug 21, 2026** — **HOOD surged +13.7% to close at $108.13** (intraday high $109.71) on a Bitcoin breakout (BTC +6.4% on the day, breaking decisively above the $60–70K range to ~$77K, best week since 2023) plus the tokenization/crypto-policy push. **Added ~$12 billion in market value** (Fool's wording: "around $12 billion"; TechStock² computes $11.7B off Friday's $97.2B closing market cap). HOOD was the **top S&P 500 gainer**, outperforming COIN and MSTR (2.1x BTC's % gain), on ~49.6M shares (1.9x average volume). Highest close since Jul 15.

5. **Context for the writer**: The tokenized-stock market itself is tiny — ~$2.5B of value worldwide across ~1.7M holders (RWA.xyz), vs Robinhood Q2'26 record revenue of **$1.31B (+32% YoY)**, platform assets **$369B**, net deposits $22B, ARPU $187 (+24%). The Fool's take: Friday's $12B pop priced in a bill that hasn't passed.
