## 1. The selloff trigger (dated)
The dated, sourced catalyst for the Oct 12 break was **capital-cost commentary from two chip CEOs**, not Zuckerberg. At the DBS Asia Future Forward Conference in Singapore on Monday Oct 12, Broadcom CEO Hock Tan said: "[What] I think might be the biggest constraint is capital," adding "There is more demand for compute infrastructure than we can supply," an imbalance he said could run through 2029 and into 2030. Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan: "If there's any challenge in the front, it's capital and the cost of capital, and then the other part is in the longer run, people want to see the ROI." (https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/12/intel-broadcom-ceos-warn-capital-crunch-could-become-ai-bottleneck.html)
Same day, CXMT (China) disclosed a denser DRAM design over the weekend; Micron fell ~3.6%, SK Hynix 10%, Samsung 7.4% (https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/13/cxmt-memory-chip-stock-rout.html).
Optics followed with no company news: "Optical networking shares began selling off on October 12," AAOI −9%, COHR −7%, LITE −7%, LYTE ETF −8% vs SPY −1% (Oct 14, https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/10/14/applied-optoelectronics-dives-9-as-ai-funding-fears-hit-optical-stocks-coherent-slides-7-lumentum-drops-7/). Oct 12 session: COHR −7%, peer set down together, "None of the three companies has issued a new press release" (https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/10/12/lumentum-falls-10-as-optics-selloff-hits-hardest-where-capacity-is-sold-out-applied-optoelectronics-drops-7-coherent-slides-7/).
Credit leg, Oct 15: CoreWeave 5-yr CDS briefly >1,140bp, last ~1,038bp (+16% w/w, +164% y/y); 2031 9.75% bond yield 15.78%; >$51B debt/obligations; Evercore ISI desk: market saying "no mas" to AI debt without operating cash flow (https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/15/coreweave-credit-default-swaps-hit-distress-levels-as-investors-sour-on-ai-debt.html). Barclays cut HPE and Dell to hold Oct 14, arguing hardware revenue strength reflects memory prices, not AI demand (https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/10/16/hewlett-packard-enterprise-falls-5-as-ai-financing-worries-extend-hardware-selloff-super-micro-sinks-7-dell-drops-3/).

## 2. Capacity / supply — company's own words
Q4 FY26 call, per Futurum: "another record bookings result, with fiscal 2027 effectively booked out, customer orders now extending into calendar 2028, and long-term agreements (LTAs) running through the end of the decade"; LTAs "typically carry rising annual volumes, minimum take-or-pay commitments, and agreed pricing for the life of the agreement"; management "sees no signs of attenuation in customer demand" (Aug 14, https://futurumgroup.com/insights/coherent-q4-fy-2026-earnings-1-6t-transceivers-ramp-cpo-revenue-approaches/).
Direct quote as reported: "Fiscal 27 is basically completely booked out," the CEO said (Oct 15, https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/10/15/coherent-has-massively-diappointed-investors-for-6-months-a-respected-analyst-predicts-it-will-double-moving-forward/).
InP is the binding constraint: Coherent made ~80% more InP lasers Y/Y in the June quarter; on track to double internal InP output by end of the Sept quarter, a quarter early, and "more than double again by the end of calendar 2027"; 6-inch yields exceed 3-inch; CFO Luther: a 6-inch wafer yields ~4x the output of a 3-inch at ~half the cost; third 6-inch site (Zurich) online H1 CY2027 (same Futurum URL). Company deck: same doubling claims plus CHIPS Act LOI up to $50M (https://www.coherent.com/content/dam/coherent/site/en/documents/investors/investor-presentations/2026/august-12/investor-presentation-20260812.pdf).
Copper-to-optics, verbatim Anderson: "As AI datacenter architectures increasingly transition from copper to optical connectivity, we believe Coherent's broad photonic technology portfolio and manufacturing scale uniquely position us to deliver accelerating growth" (Aug 12 release, https://www.coherent.com/news/press-releases/fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-year-2026-results). Also: "AI runs on compute, but it scales on optical connectivity" (Futurum URL above).
CPO traction: "more than ten CPO customer engagements and more than ten NPO engagements, with anchor customers and long-term agreements secured for both architectures" (Sept 21 PhotonLink release, https://www.coherent.com/news/press-releases/launches-photonlink-integrated-optics-platform-ai-infrastructure).

## 4. Bear case, sharpened
(a) Margin: Q1 FY27 guide is 39.5–41.5% non-GAAP; **midpoint 40.5% is +30bp vs Q4's 40.2%**, so the guide is not a stall on the midpoint — only the floor (39.5%) is below Q4. Luther reiterated a >42% target and noted GM expanded in 8 of past 9 quarters, +660bp cumulative (Futurum URL). This bear point is weaker than it looks; do not overstate it.
(b) Cash: FY26 operating cash flow **$79.5M** (vs $633.6M) against capex **$1,102.9M** (vs $440.8M) → FCF ≈ **−$1.02B**; capex was 15.5% of FY26 revenue. Q4 capex alone $556M (vs $290M Q3, $131M a year ago) = 27.2% of Q4 revenue, guided to rise again in Q1; ~18-month payback claimed; leverage 0.7x. (Cash flow: Aug 12 release PDF https://www.coherent.com/content/dam/coherent/site/en/documents/investors/financial-releases/2026/august-12/earnings-release-fy26-q4.pdf ; capex cadence: Futurum URL.) ⚠️ Coherent has **not** published a FY27 total capex figure — only "increase sequentially in Q1."
(c) Concentration: "two customers each contributing more than 10% of total revenues in fiscal 2026" (FY26 10-K, filed Aug 14, 2026, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/820318/000082031826000020/iivi-20260630.htm). NVIDIA's purchase commitment is nonexclusive (given).
(d) Industrial: Q4 $430.5M, −15.8% Y/Y; on a pro-forma basis roughly flat, with semis/display capex equipment growing and broader industrial weak (Futurum URL).
**Single falsifiable bear signal:** a Q1 FY27 (Nov 4) print where Datacenter & Comms grows ≥50% but non-GAAP GM lands at or below 39.5% (guide floor) — growth bought with price/yield loss — or any Q2 guide that fails to expand GM off 40.5%. Secondary: FY27 capex guided above ~$2B while OCF stays under ~$500M.
