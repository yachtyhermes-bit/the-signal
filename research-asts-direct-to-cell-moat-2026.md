## 3. Competitive vs Starlink Direct-to-Cell
- Starlink DTC: commercial since July 2025 (T-Mobile T-Satellite, ~$10/mo add-on); ~650 satellites; ~12M connections end-2025 → ~16M by mid-2026; messaging/apps/voice-first; 500K+ sq mi US coverage.
- Starlink V2 (mid-2027 via Starship, ~1,200 sats): 100x data density, targets 5G-class speeds — directly attacks AST's broadband differentiation.
- AST differentiation: broadband-class (peak ~200 Mbps, video-capable) on unmodified phones, 3GPP-aligned; MNO wholesale model vs SpaceX vertical integration; carriers hedge against SpaceX disintermediation via JV + AST equity stakes; Europe sovereignty angle; ironic dependency: SpaceX (Starlink owner) is AST's primary launch provider.

## 4. Moat
1) Spectrum partnerships — carriers contribute scarce licensed low-band spectrum (700/800/850 MHz). 2) Carrier alignment — investors + customers + distribution (~3B subscriber reach, near-zero CAC). 3) Technology/IP — largest commercial phased arrays in LEO (2,400 sq ft), ~200 Mbps peak, patent portfolio. 4) Vertical integration — in-house manufacturing (Midland TX, MD), production through BB-32. 5) Regulatory — FCC license + NSF/NASA coordination; FirstNet entrenchment; European sovereign positioning. 6) >$1.2B contracted backlog de-risks early revenue.

## 5. Key risks
1) Launch/execution: New Glenn failure lost BB7, 3–6 month slip; dependence on competitor SpaceX for rides; FCC milestone pressure (124 by 2030).
2) Capital intensity/dilution: ~$1.1B/yr capex, FCF -$1.2B; cash ~= convertible debt; repeated raises ($1.0B + $1.15B converts 2026, July equity raise).
3) Competition timing: Starlink V2 could close the broadband gap before AST scales.
4) Revenue timing: zero service revenue until H1 2027; FY26 revenue is gateway/gov; consensus "hold"; Q2 print 8/10.
5) Tech/regulatory: D2D broadband at scale unproven; interference/coexistence; astronomy brightness conditions; multi-country spectrum coordination.
6) Market structure: ~20% short interest of float, high beta, retail-driven volatility; key-man (CEO Abel Avellan).
