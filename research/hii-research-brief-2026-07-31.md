## 2. Q2 2026 EARNINGS STORY (reported July 30, 2026)
- **EPS $5.27 vs $3.82 consensus est (+38.0%)** — up 36.5% YoY from $3.86 (Zacks consensus was $3.79; beat by 39.1%)
- **Revenue $3.42B vs $3.15B est (+8.4%)** — up 10.9% YoY from $3.08B
- Net income $208M vs $152M a year ago; operating income $210M vs $163M
- Segmental operating income $224M vs $172M; segment op margin 5.6% (+100 bps YoY)
- Segment detail:
  - **Newport News Shipbuilding: $1.85B revenue, +15.3% YoY** — the star; aircraft carrier + submarine volumes
  - **Ingalls Shipbuilding: $845M** (up from $724M) on higher amphibious assault ship volumes; op income $58M, margin 6.9% (vs 7.5%)
  - **Mission Technologies: $760M** (slightly down YoY) but segment op income improved to $55M
- First half: revenue $6.52B (vs $5.82B), net income $357M (vs $301M)

## 3. KEY CATALYSTS
- **Backlog $57.3B** at June 30, 2026, up from $53.1B at YE2025; ~35% of remaining performance obligations converts to revenue through 2027 (decade-long contract visibility)
- **$6.7B of new contract awards in Q2**
- Newport News Shipbuilding growth +15.3% on nuclear carrier/submarine demand
- CVN-79 (USS John F. Kennedy) completed builder's trials; preliminary acceptance expected; **five ships planned for delivery over the next year**
- Company targeting **15% shipbuilding throughput improvement** in 2026 (CEO Chris Kastner: "Given the significant shipbuilding demand and our proven ability to drive higher throughput, we are pleased to increase our top line expectations...")
- Only U.S. yard that builds nuclear-powered aircraft carriers; one of only two U.S. nuclear-submarine yards (with GD's Electric Boat)

## 4. WHAT HII BUILDS (product line-up)
- **Nuclear-powered aircraft carriers** — Ford-class (CVN-78 Enterprise, CVN-79 Kennedy, CVN-80 Enterprise, CVN-81 Doris Miller) at Newport News
- **Virginia-class attack submarines** (SSN) — co-built with Electric Boat
- **Columbia-class ballistic-missile submarines** (SSBN) — Ohio-class replacement, co-built with Electric Boat
- **Amphibious assault ships** — America-class (LHA) at Ingalls
- **Arleigh Burke-class destroyers** (DDG) at Ingalls; plus future frigates/battleships mentioned on calls
- **Mission Technologies** — cyber, C5ISR, unmanned systems, nuclear environmental services (~$3B/yr segment)

## 5. GEOPOLITICAL CONTEXT
- U.S. Navy operates ~291–293 battle-force ships vs the **355-ship goal made law by the FY2018 NDAA**; Navy's May 2026 Shipbuilding Plan targets 355 by FY40
- **FY2026 defense budget = largest Navy shipbuilding request in two decades**: ~19 new battle-force ships / ~$47.3B in procurement (vs 5 ships the prior year); reports of the FY2027 request possibly doubling
- **China naval expansion** — PLAN now the world's largest navy; Indo-Pacific tensions driving urgency
- **AUKUS (Pillar 1)**: May 30, 2026 Shangri-La Dialogue announcement — Australia will acquire **three in-service Block IV Virginia-class boats** (SSN-792 to SSN-800 cohort, some still under construction) for its first SSNs, departing from the 2023 two-boat "Optimal Pathway"; SSN-AUKUS co-development with UK/Australia continues; HII hosted an Australian delegation at Newport News (July 2026) — direct demand driver for the yard; CRS: 355-ship goal includes 66 SSNs; Virginia-class costs ~$5.0B/boat at 2-per-year rate, FY26 budget requested 2 more boats
- Trump EO 14265 "Modernizing Defense Acquisitions" + FY26 NDAA direction to speed shipbuilding; new frigate/battleship programs cited as upside by HII
- Defense-budget risk: FY2027 appropriations process still ongoing; HII flags possible need for short-term funding (CR) before Oct 1, 2026

## 6. MANAGEMENT GUIDANCE (raised)
- **Shipbuilding revenue raised to $10.2B–$10.4B** (from $9.7B–$9.9B)
- **Shipbuilding operating margin floor raised to 6.0%** (range 6.0%–6.5%, from 5.5%–6.5%); Q2 shipbuilding margin actual: 6.3%
- Mission Technologies unchanged: $3.0B–$3.2B revenue, ~5% op margin
- Q3 look-ahead: shipbuilding revenue ~$2.6B, op margin ~6.3%; Mission Tech ~$760M at ~4%
- Medium-term: ~6% shipbuilding revenue growth; FCF $500–600M (company reaffirmed FCF outlook on the call)
- FY26 total revenue ≈ $13.2B–$13.6B implied

## 7. RISKS / BEAR CASE
- **Free cash flow deeply negative**: Q2 FCF **-$150M (reported; ~-$153M per some trackers) vs +$730M a year ago**; H1 FCF **-$611M** (vs +$428M H1 2025) on unfavorable trade working capital and timing of billings on Navy contracts; Q2 operating cash flow -$31M vs +$823M YoY
- Only $12M cash on hand (LT debt $2.70B); company asserts liquidity sufficient for 12 months; Fitch affirmed BBB, projected leverage ~2.3x by YE2026 (was ~3.7x in 2024)
- **Fixed-price contract exposure**: pre-pandemic fixed-price contracts lacking cost escalators still ~60% of shipbuilding revenue (Fitch, 2024) — margin/FCF drag; margin expansion depends on phasing these out
- Cost overruns/throughput risk on Columbia-class (first-of-class) and carrier programs; labor/supply-chain constraints in shipbuilding industrial base
- FY2027 defense budget uncertainty / possible CR before Oct 1, 2026
- Valuation: EV/EBITDA 13.3x is not cheap even at 19x earnings; consensus is split (7 buy / 8 hold)

## 8. SUGGESTED ARTICLE ANGLE
"America's nuclear warship monopoly just crushed earnings" — HII is the only builder of U.S. nuclear aircraft carriers and one of two submarine yards, sitting on a $57.3B backlog (~4x revenue) as the Navy races toward the 355-ship goal and AUKUS adds demand. The Q2 beat (+38% EPS, +8.4% revenue, raised guidance) is the payoff; the bear case is negative FCF (-$150M quarter, -$611M H1) and fixed-price contract drag.
