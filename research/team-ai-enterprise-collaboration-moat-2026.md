## 3. AI STRATEGY — THE MOAT (Atlassian Intelligence → Rovo + Teamwork Graph)

- **Rovo (AI assistant) adoption:** used by **80%+ of the Fortune 500**; **Rovo-assisted actions +50% QoQ**; **Rovo adopters grow ARR at 2x+ the rate of non-adopters**; AI credit usage +20% MoM (Zacks, June).
- **Teamwork Graph = the moat.** Enterprise knowledge graph weaving six contexts — Knowledge, Work, Communications, Code, Assets, People — across Jira, Confluence, Bitbucket, Loom, Teams, Slack, Google Drive, Salesforce, GitHub, Workday, etc. MCB: agents grounded in Teamwork Graph deliver **up to 44% more accurate answers while consuming 48% fewer tokens**. "Context is a clear compounding differentiator."
- **Team '26 conference (May 2026) launches:** **Agents in Jira (GA)** — Jira as the agent orchestration/control plane with audit trails; **Rovo Studio (GA)** — no-code agent/automation builder; **Rovo MCP Server + CLI (open beta)** — Teamwork Graph context usable in ANY MCP-compatible agent (Claude, Cursor, etc.); **Rovo Code Intelligence** (early access); **Jira Coding Agent** (frontier models → ready-to-review PRs); **@Jira agent for Slack**; **Agent Sessions**; **Rovo Ops** for IT ops; **Claude and Cursor in Jira**.
- **Atlassian Intelligence** = the underlying AI layer brand (search, virtual agents, content generation) that Rovo builds on; company now markets "the AI-native organization."
- Service Collection (Jira Service Management etc.) surpassed **$1B ARR, growing 30%+ YoY** — compounding AI expansion vector.
- Management: "The Teamwork Graph and AI come up in every single conversation I've had." Rovo is a "massive super cycle," not early-adopter noise.
- Positioning: results read as a **"game changer" rebuttal to the 2026 'AI kills SaaS' / SaaS-pocalypse narrative** (Yahoo Finance, Barron's, Jefferies).
- Ken Exner appointed CPO for Enterprise and Emerging; continued investment in AI, R&D, enterprise sales; DX acquisition (~1pt of FY26 growth, laps in FY27).

## 4. ARTICLE ANGLE / KEY POINTS

1. **Earnings beat + guidance beat**: $1.77B (+28%) vs $1.66B; EPS $1.87 vs $1.50; cloud re-accelerating to 31%; RPO +44% — largest-ever deal signed.
2. **AI moat = context, not models**: Teamwork Graph (25 years of enterprise work data, 350K+ orgs) makes AI answers 44% more accurate at 48% fewer tokens; Rovo in 80%+ of the Fortune 500; Rovo adopters expand ARR at 2x rate → AI is *accelerating* spend, not cannibalizing it.
3. **Moat mechanics**: switching costs (Jira/Confluence as the system of record for work), NRR >120%, $3M/$5M ARR cohorts +50%/+70%, RPO +44% = visibility; net cash balance sheet (~$1.2B debt vs ~$2.9B cash+investments), ~$1.5B TTM FCF funds the AI buildout.
4. **Bear caveats to balance**: FY27 guide decelerates (total +13%, ARR +18%) vs Q4's +28%/+23%; data center revenue shrinking ~17% (intentional migration); GAAP profitability still thin (GAAP loss, 4.5% FY27 GAAP op margin); valuation ~22x forward earnings / ~5.8x sales at $37.8B cap; AI competition (Salesforce Agentforce, Microsoft Copilot, ServiceNow).
5. **Catalyst/context**: Stock +35% to $149 on 8/7; 52-wk range $56–$184; BofA PT $175 (upgraded from Neutral); "AI kills software" debate is the macro backdrop — Atlassian is the proof point that AI can be a *demand* driver for collaboration software.
