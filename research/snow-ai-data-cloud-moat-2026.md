## 1. KEY THESIS POINTS (with data)

### T1 — Growth is REACCELERATING, not decelerating
- Product revenue growth: 26% YoY (Q1 FY26) → 30% (Q4 FY26) → **34% (Q1 FY27, $1.334B)** — first reacceleration in years.
- Q1 FY27 (ended 4/30/26): total revenue **$1.391B, +33% YoY**, beat consensus $1.33B by ~5%. Stock surged **+36%** on the print (5/27/26).
- FY27 product revenue guidance RAISED to **$5.84B (+31%)** from $5.66B (+27%); Q2 FY27 guide $1.415–1.420B (+30%).
- TTM revenue ~$5.03B (yfinance); FY26 (ended 1/31/26) revenue $4.684B (+29%: FY23 $2.07B → FY24 $2.81B → FY25 $3.63B).
- Inflection driver: **AI products driving consumption** — 7,100+ corporate accounts now run AI agents on the platform; Cortex monetization is pulling core compute consumption along (per Futurum/earnings analysis).

### T2 — The AI data pipeline is the moat amplifier
- **Snowflake is the governed control layer for agentic AI.** Summit 2026 (June 1–4, 20K+ attendees, 26+ releases) thesis: enterprise AI fails without trusted data, consistent context, secure controls — that is exactly Snowflake's position.
- Key AI stack: **Cortex AI** (Cortex Agents, Cortex Code — 150K+ users, 5–10x productivity gains), **Snowflake Intelligence** (agentic BI/deep research), **Data Science Agent**, **CoWork** (knowledge-worker agent), **CoCo** (agent app platform), **Cortex Training + Adaptive Compute** (AI infra).
- **$200M OpenAI partnership (2026):** OpenAI frontier models natively inside Cortex (functions, REST API, Intelligence, Cortex Code) with governance — data stays in the governed boundary.
- **Cortex Knowledge Extensions:** a data marketplace for AI — connects AP, USA Today, publishers into enterprise agents; turns third-party data into a monetized pipeline (data gravity for the AI era).
- Consumption elasticity: Snowflake separates storage from compute pricing → AI workload spikes convert directly to revenue.

### T3 — Structural moat: multi-cloud + governance + data gravity
- **Only major data platform natively spanning AWS, Azure, GCP + government clouds** (Databricks is functionally AWS-centric; BigQuery/Redshift single-cloud).
- **Network effects via Snowflake Marketplace** — data exchange where the data lives; AI-era extension via Knowledge Extensions.
- **Horizon Catalog / Horizon Context / AI Agent Identity / AI Security Posture Management** — governance as the agentic-AI differentiator (McKinsey: trust is the #1 agentic-era barrier).
- Switching costs: SQL ecosystem, 13,912 customers incl. **813 Forbes Global 2000**, 779 customers >$1M TTM product revenue (+29% YoY), 64 >$10M.
- Backlog: **RPO $9.21B, +38% YoY** (Q1 FY27); contracted revenue visibility ~1.8x annual product revenue.
- **NRR 126%** (up from 125% Q4 FY26, 124% Q1 FY26) — existing customers expanding ~26% organically even with zero new logos.

### T4 — Monetization & margin inflection
- Non-GAAP operating income $165.8M (11.9% margin, up from 9.0% a year ago); FY27 non-GAAP op margin guided to **13.5%** (from 12.5%).
- GAAP gross margin ~66.6% Q1 FY27; non-GAAP gross margin stabilized ~75% even as AI usage (GPU costs) scales.
- GAAP net loss narrowing: Q1 FY27 -$295.6M (-31% YoY); FY26 -$1.33B.
- **FCF-positive at scale:** FY26 FCF $1.117B (~24% margin); TTM FCF ~$1.7B (yfinance). Net cash: ~$4.6B cash+investments vs $2.77B total debt (incl. $2.3B 2025 convertibles).
- 76.8% institutional ownership; 48 analysts, consensus Strong Buy.

### T5 — Distribution machine (AWS axis)
- **$6B / 5-year AWS infrastructure commitment (5/27/26)** — largest ever; Graviton CPUs + GPUs for AI training/inference; AWS Marketplace lifetime sales surpassed **$7B**; majority of customers run on AWS.
- Hyperscaler marketplace + SaaS bundling (Salesforce, SAP, ServiceNow) is Snowflake's land-and-expand engine vs Databricks' technical-deployment-led motion.

### Bear caveats (balance the thesis)
- **Valuation is rich:** ~22.8x TTM sales, forward P/E ~122x, ~73x NTM EV/EBITDA (TIKR, June). Simply Wall St: bull case -41% undervalued vs bear case ~236% overvalued on cash-flow basis. Consensus mean target $302 is BELOW the $330 price — the stock has outrun Wall Street.
- Insider selling: $343M+ over 90 days incl. ~$46.8M by ex-CEO Slootman.
- Growth still decelerating vs FY23-24 (34% vs 100%+); Q2 guide +30% implies sequential moderation from Q1's +34%.
- Iceberg portability cuts switching costs over time.
- Q2 FY27 earnings **Sep 2, 2026** = the near-term catalyst; stock sits at 52-wk high ($330.49 vs $331.40 high).

## 2. COMPETITIVE DYNAMICS — Snowflake vs Databricks (the defining rivalry)

| Metric | Snowflake (public) | Databricks (private) |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | ~$5.0B TTM, +34% product growth | $5.4B ARR (Feb 2026, +65%) → **$6.9B ARR (June 2026, +80%)** |
| Valuation | $114.5B market cap (8/8/26) | $134B (Series L, Dec 2025); secondary chatter $165–175B |
| NRR | 126% | 140%+ |
| AI revenue | Not separately disclosed | $1.4B annualized AI products run rate |
| Customers >$1M | 779 | 800+ |
| Gross margin | ~75% non-GAAP | 80%+ subscription |
| IPO | Public | 2026 window expected (most critical AI valuation signal of the year) |

- **Division of labor:** Databricks wins engineering-heavy/AI-native buyers (lakehouse, ML, custom agents); Snowflake wins governed analytics/BI and business-user organizations. Both converging (Databricks adding SQL/governance; Snowflake adding Python/ML/agents).
- **Databricks' edge:** ~2x growth rate, higher NRR, earlier AI/ML heritage, MosaicML, $1.4B AI run rate, 140%+ NRR. Its IPO would reset the comp table and could pressure SNOW's multiple.
- **Snowflake's edge:** distribution (AWS Marketplace, SaaS bundling), multi-cloud neutrality, governance depth (Horizon), SQL simplicity, zero-ops, and the largest governed enterprise dataset installed base. Snowflake's market cap has closed from ~$58B (Feb) to $114.5B (Aug) — the valuation gap vs Databricks collapsed as the AI-data thesis reignited.
- **Iceberg is the great leveler:** Apache Iceberg v3 (both support it) wins the cross-platform open-table layer; Delta Lake is Databricks-leaning, Hudi fading. Iceberg commoditizes storage/portability → competitive battleground shifts to governance, AI features, and distribution — which favors Snowflake's positioning but erodes lock-in.
- **Other competitors:** Google BigQuery, AWS Redshift (analytics only), Microsoft Fabric (ecosystem bundling). None spans the full stack + multi-cloud like Snowflake.
