What We Do

Design And Build

Pioneering Agile Aerospace

We design, manufacture, integrate, and test all of our satellites in-house. Our agile processes allow us to rapidly iterate on our designs to incorporate new technologies and capabilities that enhance customer value.

Deploy

Proven and Reliable Launch Partners

We’ve launched hundreds of satellites since 2013 with a diverse range of providers. This approach enables us to maintain a consistent and reliable launch cadence to build and maintain our constellations while maintaining cost-effective operations.

Manage

Reliable Ground Stations

Every day we download and process vast amounts of imagery through our global network of ground stations. Our dedicated network is strategically located to increase our global reach, reduce latency, and lower operational costs. For direct access and constellation services customers, we can build and deploy dedicated, in-country ground stations optimized for their specific areas of interest.

Security First

We operate complex terrestrial and orbital computer networks and systems. To maintain integrity and security, we’ve developed processes for four independent security domains: corporate security, space segment, data pipeline, and customer delivery.

Process

Next-Generation Onboard Processing

Our newest satellites include the NVIDIA Jetson AI platform, which performs on-orbit edge computing to allow satellites to analyze data and extract key information before sending it back to Earth. Rapid processing overcomes data bottlenecks, lowers latency, and greatly accelerates your ability to get actionable insights.

AI-Enabled Processing for Advanced Analytics

We run AI and machine learning algorithms on our imagery to deliver analysis-ready data which powers time-series analysis and machine learning applications.
