## 1. BUSINESS MODEL & MOAT — competitive position

**Switchblade loitering munition family (the crown jewel):**
- SB300: 7 lb all-up, man-portable, 20+ min loiter, ~30 km range, 63 mph cruise / 100 mph dash (Greece FMS data sheet). Anti-personnel/light armor; tube-launched.
- SB300 Block 20: adds modular EFP (explosively formed penetrator) warhead.
- SB400: NEW medium-range anti-armor (~39 lb AUR, <40 lb), 70 mph loiter / 90 mph sprint; first purpose-built LM running AV's HALO C2 software. Unveiled Oct 2025; won Army LASSO prototype agreement May 4, 2026.
- SB600: anti-armor, ~40 km (25 mi) range, Javelin-class warhead. ~3,000 Block 1 units built — Block 1 is the version most used in Ukraine.
- SB600 Block 2 (Oct 13, 2025): 20% longer endurance, AI/ML ATR, encrypted M-Code GPS, Silvus MANET radios, SOCOM co-developed, maritime-hardened, multi-domain (air/land/sea launch; MQ-9 air-launch demo Oct 2025).

**Why the moat is real:**
- **Only Western loitering munition with years of large-scale combat use** (Ukraine, US Army/SOF). 700+ SB300 sent to Ukraine in 2022; SB600 flowed via USAI packages. Battlefield feedback loop drives rapid Block upgrades — a design cycle competitors can't replicate without combat data.
- **Program incumbency:** sole-source position on Army's Lethal Unmanned Systems (LUS) Directed Requirement — $990M IDIQ (Aug 2024, through Aug 2029); cumulative delivery orders ≈ $657M ($128M + $55.3M Jan '25 + $288M Feb '25 + $186M Feb '26). LASSO program of record (SB400; PoR ceiling $249M, initial $8.9M). Marines' OPF-L (one of three selected Apr 2024 w/ Anduril, Teledyne FLIR; AV receiving SB300 Block 20 orders). Replicator initiative (SB600 cited).
- **Production scale:** ~500 units/month today; new 19,000 m² FreedomWerx facility (Salt Lake City) opening late 2026/early 2027 → "several thousand" per month target. Competitors lack comparable fielded scale.
- **ISR franchise + installed base:** Raven RQ-11 — 29,000 units delivered, world's most-deployed tactical UAS; "majority of all unmanned aircraft in DoD inventory"; 45+ allied governments; common ground-control architecture + HALO C2 software = family lock-in. Blackwing (sub-launched recon variant of SB300, Navy/SOCOM). JUMP 20 VTOL (13–14 hr endurance, 185 km range; Navy COCO ISR selection Apr 2026; Italian MoD MQ-31A designation Jul 13, 2026), JUMP 20-X maritime variant, P550 (Group 2 eVTOL; $117.3M Army contract Jul 20, 2026 under Drone Dominance Initiative).

**Competitive threats (nuanced — do NOT frame as unopposed):**
- **Anduril:** Altius 600M (Taiwan deliveries completed Mar 2026; 291 units in the $360.2M Taiwan package alongside 720 SB300s); Barracuda-500M — Dept of War framework for ≥3,000 SLB-500M rounds over 3 years (Ground-Launched Low-Cost Containerized Munition) + air-launched cruise-missile framework (thousands/yr for 7 yrs); Roadrunner C-UAS; ~$60B IPO pending 2026. BUT WSJ (Nov 2025) documented reliability setbacks: Altius pulled from Ukraine ops after crashes/misses, Anvil counter-drone fire, drone-boat failures.
- **Shield AI:** V-BAT (FTUAS Inc 2 with Northrop; X-BAT engine w/ GE Jul 2026), Hivemind autonomy.
- **UVision (Israel):** Hero-120 cleared key US Army production milestone (Aug 6, 2026) — direct US Army encroachment.
- **Teledyne FLIR** (OPF-L), Elbit SkyStriker, IAI Harop, RTX Coyote (C-UAS), Kratos.
- **Structural setback to flag:** Army CANCELLED FTUAS (Aug 2025) — JUMP 20 was the only company in all three FTUAS increments, but Inc 2 finalists were Textron (Aerosonde Mk 4.8 HQ) and Griffon (Valiant), who were invited to the replacement "Brigade UAS directed requirement." HASC added $185.5M to keep FTUAS alive. JUMP 20's big Army Shadow-replacement prize is now contested; AV pivoted to Navy/maritime (JUMP 20-X), international, and P550.

## 6. ARTICLE ANGLE SUGGESTIONS (700–800 words, no exact prices)

1. **Hook:** Ukraine made the Switchblade the defining Western loitering munition; AV is the only company with combat-proven LM at production scale.
2. **Moat:** category-definer incumbency (LUS sole-source, LASSO, OPF-L, Replicator), family-of-systems + HALO software lock-in, production ramp (500 → thousands/month), 29,000 Ravens / "majority of DoD's unmanned inventory" as installed base.
3. **Tailwind:** DAWG $54.6B request, Drone Dominance $39.2B, Replicator; global LM market ~20% CAGR to ~$13B by 2030.
4. **International:** Ukraine validation → Greece/UK/France/Romania/Taiwan/Canada FMS wave + $874M FMS IDIQ + Ukrainian co-production.
5. **Honest counterweights:** SCAR/space impairment + litigation overhang, FTUAS restructure (JUMP 20 shut out of final two), Anduril's Barracuda/Altius scale push + UVision Hero-120 Army progress, GAAP losses from amortization, RBC's margin-ramp skepticism, FY27 guide deceleration.
6. **Thesis:** Structural winner in the drone-warfare build-out trading at a discount to its January peak because of a space-contract blowup — the moat is in loitering munitions, not space; watch the Sept 2 Q1 print and FY27 bookings for confirmation.
