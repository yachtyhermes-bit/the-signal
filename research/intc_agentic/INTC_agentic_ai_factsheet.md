**Prepared:** 24 September 2026 · **Ticker:** INTC (Intel Corporation, Nasdaq)
**Angle:** Agentic-AI CPU demand (kicked off in the public mind by Meta's Muse agent) has re-rated Intel. The swing factor from here is whether Intel names a major external 14A foundry customer.
**House rule reminder:** NO exact stock prices in the published article. Express moves as percentages only. Raw price / market-cap values below are for the writer's stats card and metadata, not for prose.

## 2. The agentic-AI CPU-to-GPU ratio claim

**2.1 — Intel's own ratio statement (original source)**
- **Statement:** On Intel's Q1 2026 earnings call, CFO **David Zinsner** described data-center CPU:GPU ratios as having already moved from 1:8 to 1:4, with the ratio able to converge to 1:1 — or tilt further toward CPUs — as workloads migrate to inference and agentic AI.
- **Numbers:** 1:8 → **1:4 today** → potentially **1:1** (or more CPU-heavy).
- **Source:** Tom's Hardware, "CPU requirements for AI workloads are multiplying… Intel already shifting production from consumer chips to Xeon…," 24 Apr 2026, reporting Intel's Q1 2026 earnings call (23 Apr 2026). Zinsner quote in that piece: "As you think about the growth rate now going forward, it's [CPU demand] going to become a significant part of the AI [total addressable market]."
- **URL:** https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/cpus/shifting-need-for-cpus-in-ai-workloads-drives-intensifying-shortages-price-hikes
  Companion (same call, Intel's results): https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/cpus/intel-stock-jumps-28-percent-setting-a-record-after-it-posts-strong-q1-with-rising-forecasts-intel-says-yields-are-improving-faster-than-expected-with-new-nodes
- **Tag:** **CONFIRMED** (company earnings call, named outlet, named executive).

**Note on the task framing:** the brief's "roughly 1:4 toward 1:1" is accurate — Intel's position is that 1:4 is *already here* and 1:1 is the direction of travel. Older formulations in the press say "1:8 toward 1:1." Use "1:4 today, heading toward 1:1" for precision.

- **Quote (exact):** "CPU to GPU ratio for training used to 1:8. And now, because of the inference, agentic AI, and more agents you have to manage, CPU is actually better, and that ratio has become 1:4 and in some cases 1:1. That's a huge opportunity for me." — **Lip-Bu Tan**, Intel CEO.

- **Statement:** Speaking at a **Deutsche Bank investor conference** (reported 2 September 2026), Intel CFO **Dave Zinsner** said customer discussions have moved beyond evaluating process technology and are increasingly about how much production capacity Intel can supply. ## 9. Editorial guardrails for this piece

2. **Muse figures are estimates.** Attribute downloads/DAU to Sensor Tower (via CNBC) or Apptopia (via TechCrunch) and say explicitly they are third-party estimates that Meta has not confirmed.

That tension is the honest frame for the bear case; the 300%-plus move off the 52-week low is the bull's.

This piece leads with the agentic-AI CPU demand re-rating and the 14A anchor-customer question — do not re-litigate the HBM4E deal or recap the earnings print.
