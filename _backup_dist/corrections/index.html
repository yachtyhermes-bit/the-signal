Corrections Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026 · Last updated: September 23, 2026

We correct errors publicly and promptly. If The Signal gets something wrong, the article is fixed and the change is recorded here.

How corrections work

  • Factual errors are corrected in the article and logged below with the date, what changed, and why.
  • Price and market data carry “as of” timestamps. When data is refreshed, the previous value and the date of the change are noted.
  • Claims are held to primary sources. If a claim cannot be verified against a filing, a company statement, or another primary record, it is not published as fact.
  • Reader-reported errors go to the top of the queue.

How to report an error

Email hello@readthesignal.net with the article link and the sentence you believe is wrong. If you have it, include the primary source — a filing, an earnings-call transcript, or a company release. Every report is read by a human.

What a correction looks like

Corrections are recorded below, newest first, as: date · article · what was wrong · what it now says. Where a correction changes the substance of a claim, the article notes it in place rather than silently editing.
