Absolutely. Free is yours forever. Upgrade to Premium or Pro whenever you're ready — your data and settings carry over seamlessly.

Yes. Premium includes a 7-day free trial, Pro a 14-day trial. Cancel before it ends and you won't be charged.

All major credit/debit cards via Stripe, plus Apple Pay and Google Pay.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No lock-in contracts. Cancel from your Account settings and you'll retain access until the end of your billing period.
