## 3. PLAIN-ENGLISH COMPANY EXPLAINER (for the Writer)
D-Wave (Palo Alto, est. 1999) is the **world's first commercial supplier of quantum computers** and the only company offering **both** quantum approaches: **quantum annealing** (its original specialty) and gate-model (added via the Jan 2026 **$550M acquisition of Quantum Circuits Inc.** — "dual-platform").
- **Quantum annealing in plain English:** instead of the "quantum bits flipping logic gates" of IBM/IonQ-style machines, an annealer encodes a **find-the-best-answer problem** (like "route 10,000 trucks with minimum fuel") into the physical energy landscape of its qubits, then lets physics settle into the **lowest-energy state = best solution**. It's purpose-built for **optimization**: logistics routing, workforce/production scheduling, resource allocation, materials science, portfolio optimization — the NP-hard combinatorial problems classical computers choke on.
- **What they sell:** (1) **Quantum systems/hardware** — the 4,500-qubit **Advantage2™** annealing systems sold outright (e.g., $20M sale to Florida Atlantic University; Davidson Technologies for U.S. defense), plus gate-model systems coming; (2) **Leap™ quantum cloud service** — real-time, on-demand quantum access over the cloud (QCaaS) where most commercial customers run production apps; (3) **Ocean™ SDK/software tools**; (4) **D-Wave Launch™** onboarding program and professional services.
- **Who buys:** enterprises (AT&T, NTT DOCOMO, Unisys, Shionogi, Nasdaq Verafin, Oki Electric), universities, government/defense (U.S. DoD programs, Canadian NRC, NSF).

## 4. WHY IT MATTERS TO AI (for the Writer)
- **Complementary compute layer:** GPUs crush training/inference but are poor at combinatorial optimization. Annealing solves the "find the best arrangement" problems that sit atop AI-era operations (supply chains, scheduling, routing, portfolio construction) — a natural coprocessor to the GPU/AI buildout, not a replacement.
- **Quantum = next compute frontier:** D-Wave's own roadmap targets **"quantum AI applications" on a 100-logical-qubit system by 2032**, and its production QCaaS usage is compounding (37.3% of QCaaS revenue is production-grade vs 9.8% a year ago). BMO's thesis: D-Wave is the rare pure-play already converting quantum into **real enterprise revenue** — the sector's lab-to-commercial crossing.
