## 5. INDEX METHODOLOGY — Indxx U.S. Infrastructure Development Index

### 5a. Eligibility (initial universe)
- Primary listing in the United States.
- Minimum total market cap **$300 million**.
- Minimum average daily turnover **>$1 million** over last 6 months (or since IPO for "Significant IPOs").
- Traded on **90% of eligible trading days** in last 6 months.
- Minimum free float **10%** of shares outstanding.
- Price < $10,000 (existing constituents grandfathered).
Source: https://pdfs.indexmethodhub.com/indxx/us-infrastructure-development/Indxx-U.S.-Infrastructure-Development-Index-Methodology_v2026.04_en.pdf

### 5b. Thematic screen ("how the sausage is made")
- Selection via Indxx's **ITICS** (Indxx Thematic Industry Classification System) plus revenue analysis: a company qualifies if it "derives a significant portion of its revenues" from the themes **or** has "stated its primary business" to be in them — i.e. a qualitative, index-provider-discretionary judgement, not a hard percentage.
- Four themes: (1) Construction & Engineering Services, (2) Raw Materials and Composites, (3) Products and Equipment, (4) Industrial Transportation.
- **U.S. revenue screen: >50% of revenue from the U.S. as of the selection date** (new entrants).
Source: https://pdfs.indexmethodhub.com/indxx/us-infrastructure-development/Indxx-U.S.-Infrastructure-Development-Index-Methodology_v2026.04_en.pdf ; https://assets.ctfassets.net/9gigg57dwmx9/funds-documents-pave-index-methodology-summary-473ad02bc7/8c8e0f0789d12879fdc8f76da9077ec4/Index-Methodology-Summary.pdf

### 5c. Selection
- Final index = **top 100 infrastructure development companies by market capitalization** (fewer if fewer qualify).
- Ranking metric: full market cap, descending. No sector-neutrality constraint (industry composition is whatever the ranking produces).

### 5d. Weighting
- **Modified market-cap weighting** on security-level market cap.
- **Single-security cap 3.0%; minimum weight floor 0.3%.**
- Anything ≥3% is set to 3%; anything ≤0.3% is floored at 0.3%; the balance is redistributed proportionately among remaining names while respecting the caps.
- No explicit sector cap.
Source: https://pdfs.indexmethodhub.com/indxx/us-infrastructure-development/Indxx-U.S.-Infrastructure-Development-Index-Methodology_v2026.04_en.pdf

### 5e. Reconstitution / rebalance schedule
- **Semi-annual.** New portfolio effective **at the close of the last trading day of January and July** ("Effective Day").
- **Selection Day** = close of the nearest Friday falling at least **one month before** the Effective Day; the selection list is built on that day's data.
- Weights calculated at the close of the **seventh trading day prior (six trading days prior) to the Effective Day**; index shares are frozen at those weights.
- Buffer rules to cut turnover: existing constituent retained if market cap ≥ **80%** of the prior limit; 6-month ADTV ≥ **70%** of prior liquidity limit; retained if still in the **top 120 by market cap** even if outside the top 100; retained if **>40%** of revenue is U.S. (vs 50% for new entrants).
- Ad-hoc deletion for delisting, prolonged suspension, acquisition, bankruptcy, or Index Committee decision. Index calculated on all weekdays (no holiday calendar).
Source: https://pdfs.indexmethodhub.com/indxx/us-infrastructure-development/Indxx-U.S.-Infrastructure-Development-Index-Methodology_v2026.04_en.pdf

### 5f. 2026 methodology changes (verified announcement)
- **Effective November 28, 2025:** amended Section 1.7 (Reconstitution and Rebalancing Rules) and added the 40% U.S.-revenue buffer for existing constituents (Section 1.6.4).
- **April 2, 2026:** methodology document amended to reference ITICS classification for theme selection (v2026.04).
Sources: https://indxx-prod.gumlet.io/prod/media/20251128T120520640Z-530421-announcement-of-amended-methodology-of-indxx-us-infrastructure-development-index-methodology.pdf ; https://pdfs.indexmethodhub.com/indxx/us-infrastructure-development/Indxx-U.S.-Infrastructure-Development-Index-Methodology_v2026.04_en.pdf

## 11. THE BEAR CASE — evidence

1. **The theme has already broken down, and recently.** Q3 2026: PAVE −10.9% vs SPY +2.4%. 1Y: +11.02% (NAV) vs SPY +16.45%. YTD: +8.73% vs SPY +14.09%. −11.5% from the 6/25/2026 peak. Sources: https://etf.net/news/infrastructure-etfs-lag-stocks-2026-10-20 ; https://www.globalxetfs.com/funds/pave ; Signal calc.
2. **The 5-year record is good, but the edge is narrowing and hasn't survived the last 12 months.** 5Y annualized +16.43% (NAV, 9/30/26) vs SPY +12.76% (+2.3pt/yr); but same-date 3Y is +23.47% vs SPY +23.94%, 1Y is +10.98% vs +16.45%, and YTD +8.73% vs +14.09% (Signal calc, 10/21/2026). The 5-year edge was earned before the current drawdown. Sources: https://www.globalxetfs.com/funds/pave ; Signal calc.
3. **Valuation is no longer cheap.** Fund P/E 28.61 trailing / 22.12 forward, P/B 3.51 vs SPY 24.55 trailing. Top names: Parker-Hannifin 33.6x, Emerson 35.9x, Fastenal 42.7x, Howmet 44.4x, Quanta 69.0x (trailing, 10/21/2026). Sources: https://www.globalxetfs.com/funds/pave ; Signal calc via yfinance.
4. **It is a cyclical capex fund wearing an infrastructure label: ~95% industrials + materials, 3.45% utilities.** Not a defensive, cash-yield infrastructure portfolio — that is IFRA (41.9% utilities, 1.79% SEC yield) at 0.30%. Sources: https://assets.globalxetfs.com/funds/documents/pave/Fact-Sheet_PAVE.pdf ; https://etf.net/news/infrastructure-etfs-lag-stocks-2026-10-20 ; https://www.ishares.com/us/products/294315/ishares-u-s-infrastructure-etf
5. **The label is looser than investors think.** Holdings include Builders FirstSource (lumber/trusses), TopBuild (insulation installation), Fastenal (fastener distribution), Wesco (electrical distribution), Ryerson (metals distribution), Herc/United Rentals (equipment rental) — housing and industrial-cycle exposures, not federal infrastructure. 10.28% of the fund is in Irish/Swiss-domiciled issuers (Eaton, Trane, CRH, Amrize). Caterpillar — the most obvious "infrastructure" stock — is not in the portfolio. Sources: https://assets.ctfassets.net/9gigg57dwmx9/funds-documents-pave-prospectus-regulatory-q1-complet-90c8e4b214/eb2f1c35faaaab602adb69998b05c19e/Q1-Complete-Holdings.pdf ; https://stockanalysis.com/etf/pave/holdings/
6. **Mechanical caps force it to sell its winners, on a calendar everyone can see.** The 3% cap / 0.3% floor means no position can compound past ~3%; weights are struck six trading days before the last trading day of January and July, with the selection list fixed a month earlier. That is a structural headwind in a momentum-driven capex cycle and a predictable rebalance flow. Source: https://pdfs.indexmethodhub.com/indxx/us-infrastructure-development/Indxx-U.S.-Infrastructure-Development-Index-Methodology_v2026.04_en.pdf
7. **Crowding: money is arriving as performance leaves.** +$2.68B YTD inflows into a $13.6B fund (+20% organic) while the price fell ~11% from its June high; AUM +37.7% in the six months to 5/31/2026. Sources: https://etfthemes.com/insights/thematic-pulse-investors-keep-funding-infrastructure-as-ai-financing-comes-under-strain/ ; §3.
8. **Cost and income.** 0.47% vs 0.30% for IFRA and 0.0945% for SPY; 30-day SEC yield 0.55% vs IFRA 1.79%; 5Y tracking difference −0.63%/yr, worse than the fee. Sources: §2, §7, §9.
9. **Policy/tariff dependence.** Deere (3.44%) booked $110M of tariff recoveries in Q3 FY26 and $382M over nine months, and calls FY2026 "the bottom of the ag equipment cycle" — the top holdings are exposed to trade policy and to a farm-capex trough. Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/315189/000110465926098904/de-20260820xex99d1.htm
