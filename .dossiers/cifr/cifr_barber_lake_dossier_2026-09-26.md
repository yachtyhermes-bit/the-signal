- **The remaining halls need to be delivered Q4'26–Q1'27 so rent can fully ramp**, per the PR's own "Cipher remains on track with the revised delivery schedule." Every quarter of slip pushes both Fluidstack rent and the start of the 20-year clock to the right, while the 7.125% Cipher Compute notes ($1,733M) amortize **rent-based**.
- **Black Pearl / Wink has to keep performing as the proof point.** It is now the only HPC asset actually collecting rent: first AWS capacity delivered **August 2026, two months early** (colomap 200), and rent commenced. Barber Lake is the second.

The one piece of credit support in the story — **Google warrants backstopping the Barber Lake Fluidstack lease** (cryptoslate 200) — attaches to the *first* lease, not necessarily the second.

- **AI capex has to still be a thing in the late 2030s**, when the second lease's rent is the one being collected. Company contracted revenue stood at ~$11.4B pre-deal (Q2'26 deck) and ~$16.6B pro forma after the $5.2B (derived: 11.4 + 5.2).

- **$359.3M of overrun exposure sits on Cipher.** = 63.9% of book equity ($562.057M) and 1.88x TTM revenue ($191.093M). Above it, only 50% is reimbursed, over 20 years, as rent (PR).
- **Leverage.** Total debt $6,016M vs equity $562.057M = **10.70x**; liabilities 92.17% of assets; net debt $1,456M on the company's own basis (which nets $3,728M of *restricted, project-level* cash) or $4,754.971M on the yfinance basis against $831.829M of unrestricted cash. Sub-investment-grade ratings: Ba3/BB- (Barber Lake notes) and Ba2/BB- (Black Pearl, Stingray).
- **Amortization is rent-linked, and rent starts with delivery.** Q2'26 deck footnote 2: the 7.125% Cipher Compute LLC notes "amortize[] in line with the Google backstop, which is determined by rent payments made by Fluidstack" (stocktitan 403/alive). *(The brief's framing that amortization "does not begin until Barber Lake is complete" is imprecise — the disclosed mechanic ties it to rent payments, not to a completion event.)*
- **The second lease's rent does not begin until roughly the late 2030s** (thelatent 200). Ten years of construction, operating and interest cost precede it, and today's debt must be serviced from the first lease only.
- **Cash generation is negative and the top line is shrinking.** TTM FCF **-$1,496.008M** (OCF -$256.490M, capex -$1,239.518M). Revenue fell from $71.707M (Q3'25) to $24.837M (Q2'26), **-65.36%**, because the pivot is killing the mining business. Q2'26 had a $150.510M non-cash warrant charge; Q4'25 net loss was $734.205M.
- **Interest is already bigger than the remaining operating business.** H1 2026 interest expense $66.7M vs Q2'26 mining revenue $24.837M = **2.69x**.
- **The equity story now depends on a financed AI build-out that Cipher is funding with debt and asset sales.** 1,619 BTC sold in H1 2026 at a **$47.7M realized loss**; 646 BTC left ($37.8M) vs 1,433 at 12/31/25; management has said it will liquidate the remainder in 2026. H1'26 buying of property and equipment: $964.261M, funded by $2.84B of net financing inflows including $129.2M of at-the-market stock sales.
