## 0. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH FACT BASE

Akamai disclosed after the Sept 24, 2026 close that Anthropic has committed to pay it **approximately $11.6 billion over seven years** for dedicated cloud computing capacity and managed support, delivering **CPU (central processing unit) workloads** across Akamai Cloud. The deal can expand by **up to an additional $9 billion**, for a **total potential commitment of roughly $20 billion**. Akamai granted Anthropic a **warrant on non-voting convertible Series B preferred stock representing ~7.7 million common shares, or up to ~5% of Akamai's common stock outstanding, at an exercise price of $111.33/share**. Akamai expects **~$5.5 billion of capital expenditure** tied to the agreement and an **~$1.7 billion increase in 2026 capex** to pre-buy supply-chain components including memory, with **no impact to 2026 revenue guidance**. Shares surged **~20–22% in after-hours trading Sept 24**; they opened Friday **+13.6%** and the rally faded intraday.

## 2. THE CONTRACT — TERM-BY-TERM

### 2.1 Structure *(8-K, Item 1.01)*
- On **September 18, 2026**, Akamai and **Anthropic, PBC** entered into **Project Plan 2 and Project Plan 3** under their existing **Master Services Agreement dated May 5, 2026**.
- Akamai provides "dedicated cloud computing capacity and related managed support services."
- **~$11.6B aggregate**, "subject to any termination described below and satisfaction of certain delivery and service availability requirements."
- **Each Project Plan has an initial seven-year term** commencing on its respective service start date *(note: two plans, two start dates — both seven years)*.
- Akamai declared the MSA material "because the Anthropic MSA is no longer immaterial in amount or significance to the Company."
- Filing date: **Sept 24, 2026**; the underlying execution date: **Sept 18, 2026**. The public announcement is **Sept 24 after the close**.

### 2.2 Termination rights *(8-K, Item 1.01)*
- Akamai may terminate the MSA on uncured breach by Anthropic.
- Anthropic may terminate on material uncured breach by Akamai, **or on a change of control of Akamai in favor of a direct competitor of Anthropic**.
- Either party may terminate on bankruptcy/insolvency/receivership of the other, or if no project plan remains in effect.
- **Anthropic may terminate each Project Plan upon notice of a material outage**, subject to conditions.
- Carve-out: if the MSA is terminated for breach, non-breaching project plans survive as separate agreements.

### 2.3 Expansion option *(press release + Reuters)*
- Potential expansion "by up to an additional **$9 billion**," "which represents a total potential commitment of approximately **$20 billion**."
- Each additional **$3 billion** of cloud services requires "mutually agreed upon terms" — i.e. the $9B is **not committed**, it is optional.
- Reuters: "The seven-year contract could expand by an additional $9 billion, bringing the potential total commitment to about $20 billion."

### 2.4 CPU / Akamai Cloud *(press release + Bloomberg)*
- Press release bullet: "**Anthropic to leverage Akamai Cloud's distributed infrastructure and software to support CPU workload growth at scale.**"
- Bloomberg (Lynn Doan): "Akamai will supply Anthropic access to **central processing units, or CPUs**, a type of generalist chip that's seen renewed demand in data centers where it's helping support AI services."
- Press release on the platform: "Akamai Cloud supports a continuum of compute from core to edge, with a vastly distributed network spanning **thousands of points of presence**… built with **diversified hardware**."

**Key structural facts a writer must not miss:**
- **$111.33 was the 30-trading-day VWAP**, not a negotiated discount. The 8-K states the price "represent[ed] the volume-weighted average price of the Company's common stock… over the 30 consecutive trading days immediately preceding the Issue Date, multiplied by 20." MarketWatch notes $111.33 is "near to Thursday's closing price."
- **The warrant is issued in consideration of Anthropic's entry into Project Plan 3** — not a cash payment (8-K, Item 3.02). Exempt under **Section 4(a)(2)**, no underwriter, no commissions.
- **Cash-settled exercise only**: "Exercises of the Warrant must be settled by Anthropic's payment to the Company of the aggregate exercise price per Warrant Share **in cash**." No cashless exercise.
- **No voting rights** — except as required by Delaware law. Liquidation preference just **$0.01/share**; thereafter participates with common on an as-converted basis. Dividend rights mirror common on an as-converted basis.
- **Auto-conversion only on transfer** outside Anthropic and its wholly owned subsidiaries. Anthropic **cannot** elect to convert. Series B is transferable only to Anthropic and wholly owned subs.
- **"A portion of the warrant representing approximately 2% of Akamai's common stock outstanding is expected to vest in connection with today's announced $11.6 billion commitment. The remaining approximately 3% would vest throughout the successful expansion of the commitment up to an additional $9 billion within the seven-year term of the warrant. Each additional $3 billion purchase of cloud services, at mutually agreed upon terms, will result in the vesting of approximately 1% of Akamai's common stock outstanding."** (press release)
- **Termination condition**: vesting is conditioned on "the Anthropic MSA remain[ing] in effect" (8-K).
