# VERIFIED RESEARCH BRIEF — Broadcom (AVGO) / Anthropic $42B chip-lease financing
Date: Oct 1, 2026. Source of truth: Reuters EXCLUSIVE, Oct 1 2026 (via syndication: Miami Herald, The Daily Star, TradingView, pulse2).

## The story
- Anthropic's IPO prospectus reveals: **Broadcom has agreed to lend Anthropic up to $42 BILLION** to finance infrastructure spending. The instruments are **convertible** — Broadcom could designate a financing partner, and the notes could convert into Anthropic shares.
- Anthropic said it **does not expect any notes to be sold before it completes its IPO**.
- The convertible note **could finance about one-third of the $125.2 BILLION commitment** Anthropic has made for a **five-year lease of TPU computing capacity**.
- Compute = **Google TPUs, supplied by Google and Broadcom, beginning in 2027**.
- **Anthropic is expected to become the largest customer in Broadcom's chip-design (XPU/custom ASIC) business in 2027.** Broadcom Q3 FY26 call wording: "on track to become our largest XPU customer in 2027 and sustain that in 2028."
- Context: Anthropic announced an expanded partnership with Broadcom + Google in **April 2026** → multiple gigawatts of next-gen TPU capacity starting 2027. Broadcom's **Form 8-K filed 04/06/2026** quantifies "approximately **3.5 gigawatts**" as part of that.
- **April 2026**: Anthropic deposited cash into a **restricted account for Broadcom's benefit**, and may be required to contribute more.
- Anthropic warns that **certain payment or performance defaults could make a substantial portion of lease obligations immediately due while limiting its ability to use the $42B facility** to cover those payments.
- The prospectus flags **"potential conflicts of interest"** from Broadcom supplying hardware AND acting as financing partner, and says Broadcom's pricing/hardware decisions could affect Anthropic's access to compute.
- Anthropic's IPO **could value it around $2 trillion** (Reuters framing: could; some coverage cites ~$1.5T) — treat as expectation, not fact.

## Bear case (checkable, short-seller framing)
1. **Customer concentration**: Broadcom's multi-year visibility leans on a handful of XPU customers; Anthropic is to be #1 in 2027-28 and OpenAI #2. CEO Hock Tan has acknowledged Google is "looking elsewhere."
2. **Circular financing**: Broadcom lends Anthropic up to $42B to lease Broadcom-designed chips, and separately backstops the Apollo/Blackstone SPV up to $29B on the first tranche. The supplier is financing the customer's purchase — the "reciprocal spending" pattern AI skeptics cite.
3. **Counterparty credit**: Anthropic is pre-IPO; the lease obligations (~$161.2B of Broadcom-related leases per its prospectus, largely non-cancelable except on default) dwarf its disclosed cash/STI. Broadcom's backstop plus rack residual value are the cushion if Anthropic can't pay.
4. **Convertible dilution / accounting optics**: a $42B convertible note converting into Anthropic equity is equity risk on Broadcom's books; meanwhile AVGO trades ~44× trailing vs ~18× forward earnings, and gross margin slipped QoQ on AI mix (bulls are told to stop focusing on gross margin).
