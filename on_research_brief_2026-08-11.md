## 4. AI DATA CENTER SUPPLY CHAIN POSITION (picks-and-shovels core)
- ON makes **power management chips for AI servers**: MOSFETs, SiC, IGBTs, drivers/controllers — the full "power tree" from high-voltage rack infrastructure to low-voltage delivery next to the processor
- **Design wins: NVIDIA MGX ecosystem, Great Wall (China cloud/AI), AWS** (power supply + battery backup)
- **800V DC distribution architecture** = next content leg (ramp late 2027/early 2028); MPWR also sampling 800V AC-DC products
- Demand so strong ON **prioritized AI DC shipments over auto/industrial** in Q2; lead times ~32 weeks; book-to-bill >1; 2nd round of price increases
- Management: AI DC is "fastest-growing business"; Analyst Day **Sept 16** to detail the DC opportunity (Morgan Stanley catalyst call)
- **Synaptics deal (announced June 25)**: all-stock, 1.35 ON shares/SYNA share, ~$7B EV, expands TAM by $30B to $243B by 2030, moves ON from AI DC power into "Physical AI" (robotics/edge); close expected mid-2027; ~$200M synergies; six firms (Deutsche Bank, TD Cowen, Barclays, Susquehanna) downgraded SYNA to Hold post-deal
