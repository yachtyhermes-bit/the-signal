## THE ONE CORE TENSION
Oracle's AI franchise has never looked better on paper — $664B RPO, IaaS +121%, FY27 revenue guided to +34% — while the machine that funds it is a single counterparty (OpenAI, ~half of that backlog per S&P) drawing on a balance sheet that burned $5.4B of free cash flow last quarter against $155.9B of debt rated one notch above junk. The force majeure notice is the first public evidence Oracle will use contract language to protect its own cash if Project Jupiter slips — which helps Oracle's income statement but tells the ~20 banks holding 89–91 cent project paper that the anchor tenant may not absorb the delay.

**Bull framing (source-backed):** RPO +$209B YoY and +$26B QoQ "does not require additional cash from Oracle" (vast majority of new contracts prepay/BYOH); $664B vs StreetAccount $630.6B; Project Jupiter does not affect FY27 guidance (co-CEO Clay Magouyrk, 9/10); Oracle: "Project Jupiter remains on our planned schedule" (to CNBC); Blue Owl: "This notice does not change the financial commitments to this multi-year project."
**Bear framing (source-backed):** debt at 89–91¢ and syndication stalled; S&P downgrade 7/9 to BBB–, ~$42B FY27 FCF deficit forecast, mid-4x adjusted leverage; ~$260B of data-center leases signed; NM State Land Office denied the pipeline twice; no contingency power plan admitted on the record ("I don't know how to power the data center" — Oracle VP Mahesh Thiagarajan, at a Doña Ana County commission meeting).

## UNVERIFIED / RISKY CLAIMS

1. **The force majeure notice itself is a report, not an Oracle confirmation.** Sourced to "people familiar with the situation." Oracle's only on-record statement is "Project Jupiter remains on our planned schedule… We are fully committed to New Mexico and are confident in our path forward." Reuters explicitly noted it "could not independently verify" the report. CNBC "confirmed" only that the notice went to a Blue Owl unit. **Attribute carefully.**
2. **"A $400 billion deal with Oracle and SoftBank last year" (Bloomberg Law)** — imprecise. The $400B figure is total Stargate investment across five new sites + Abilene + CoreWeave (OpenAI/SoftBank, 9/23/2025). The Oracle–OpenAI cloud contract is **>$300B over ~5 years** (WSJ 9/10/2025; SoftBank release). Do not describe a $400B Oracle/OpenAI contract.
3. **Capacity figure varies:** 2.45 GW (Bloomberg/FP/Bloomberg Law) vs "as much as 2.5 GW" of Bloom fuel cells (Bloomberg via Energy Connect) vs "more than a gigawatt" (STACK press release, 9/26/2025) vs 4.5 GW for the whole Oracle–OpenAI Stargate agreement. Use **2.45 GW** with Bloomberg attribution and note the range.
4. **"Oracle credit-risk gauge hit a record high Thursday"** — single-sourced to Bloomberg Law; no instrument or level given. Corroborated directionally: Startup Fortune (CDS record Aug 4), Times of India ("near 18-year high"). Do not state a CDS level.
5. **Oracle YTD –31% (Bloomberg Law)** — computed YTD is **–28.5%** at $137.94 and **–30.9%** at the $133.48 low. Bloomberg's number reflects an intraday print. Use the computed figure or say "about 30%."
6. **Blue Owl –3.6% (Bloomberg Law)** — my yfinance snapshot shows OWL ~–4.4% intraday. Directionally consistent; don't hard-quote –3.6% as a close.
7. **Reuters 8/4 "Oracle debt of $129.5B ≈ 4.3x EBITDA"** conflicts with the reported balance sheet: total debt was **$156.19B at 5/31/2026** and **$155.93B at 8/31/2026** (yfinance); S&P is cited at **$167B**.
