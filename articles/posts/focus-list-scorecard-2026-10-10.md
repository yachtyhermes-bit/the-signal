### 5. The single most-argued question in the market right now
**Can the AI capex boom — and the increasingly debt-funded financing behind it — keep carrying the S&P 500 and Nasdaq to record highs while the Fed still leans toward one more hike and the 10-year sits near 5.25%, its highest in over two decades?** The indexes hit records on Oct 6 on Nvidia/AMD strength, but gave part of it back on Oct 7–8 the moment the 30-year climbed to 5.71% and oil topped $100 — showing how tightly the rally is now tethered to the cost of capital.
