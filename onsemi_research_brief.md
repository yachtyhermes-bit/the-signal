## 4. AI DATA CENTER ANGLE (the story)

- **AI data center revenue now expected to MORE THAN DOUBLE in 2026** (raised from "double" guided in May) — "AI data center remains our fastest-growing business"
- **SiC (silicon carbide) revenue in AI data centers expected +~60% YoY in 2026**
- **NVIDIA MGX ecosystem**: onsemi expanded role, supplying advanced power systems for next-gen AI data centers
- **New design wins**: 2 power-supply platform wins with **Great Wall** (China cloud/AI infra provider); **AWS** power-supply and battery-backup system wins
- **"Power tree" thesis**: opportunity spans the full AI power chain — from high-voltage infrastructure to low-voltage delivery near processors — "not limited to silicon carbide products"
- **800V DC distribution architecture**: next catalyst for higher semiconductor content; ramp expected late 2027/early 2028, gradual build
- Demand is so strong onsemi **prioritized AI data center shipments over automotive/industrial** during Q2; expects manufacturing to catch up in Q3/Q4; no material customer impact
- **Supply tightening**: lead times ~32 weeks (from ~27); book-to-bill >1 for several quarters; second round of price increases to offset raw-material/external-mfg costs (applies across markets incl. automotive; no softer pricing expected in 2027)
- Context: Q1 2026 AI data center revenue was already up >30% QoQ; Treo platform revenue up 2.5x sequentially in Q1; GaN design funnel >$1.5B (GaNEXUS portfolio launched; GaN revenue begins 2027)

## 8. CATALYSTS / STRATEGIC ITEMS

- **Synaptics acquisition** (proposed): connected-compute capabilities complementing power/sensing/control; expected close mid-2027
- **FabRight restructuring**: divesting Mountain Top (PA) and Philippines fabs; ~$35M annualized savings (initial benefits 2027, full 2028)
- **Utilization 83%** in Q2 (from 77% Q1); each +1pt utilization ≈ 25–30 bps gross margin (two-quarter lag) → margin expansion runway
- **Rivian R2 content wins**: MOSFETs for zonal power distribution + SiC for onboard charging
- Second half 2026 expected to outgrow first half (management: "not flattish — good outlook")
- $300M of non-core revenue exits during 2026 (incl. $35M in Q2) — headline growth is "cleaner" than it looks
