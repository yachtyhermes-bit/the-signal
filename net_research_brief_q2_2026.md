## 2. GUIDANCE RAISE
- **Q3 FY26:** revenue $736–737M (+31% YoY; 2.1% above consensus), non-GAAP op income $129–130M, non-GAAP EPS $0.34
- **FY26 revenue:** raised to **$2.864–2.870B** (midpoint $2.867B) from $2.805–2.813B (midpoint $2.809B) — **+$60M midpoint raise (+2.1%)**, implies ~32% FY growth; above analyst avg of $2.81B
- **FY26 non-GAAP op income:** raised to $443–445M (from $418–421M) — restructuring savings flowing to bottom line
- **FY26 non-GAAP EPS:** raised to **$1.25–1.26** (from $1.19–1.20), +5%
- Restructuring charges FY26: up to $165M total ($130M cash); Prince says GAAP profitability "pacing ahead" of end-2028 target; Q2 would've been near GAAP break-even ex-restructuring

## 3. EARNINGS CALL HIGHLIGHTS (Zacks + Investing.com transcript summaries)
- Prince: "fundamental rewrite of the Internet for machine-to-machine traffic" — agentic internet thesis; more than half of network traffic now nonhuman
- **New products:** Monetization Gateway, wallets, cloudflare.pay (agent-driven commerce rails); research pilot with OpenAI re: AI/content-owner economics
- Workers now a "meaningful revenue contributor" (beyond adoption phase); pool-of-funds contracts incorporating Workers
- Big win: pool-of-funds Workers deal — customer building AI agents chose Cloudflare over hyperscalers (threat intelligence preventing compute abuse, innovation pace, FedRAMP)
- **CFO Seifert caution:** shift from ratable SaaS toward pool-of-funds/consumption/"T-shirt" structures = quarter-to-quarter revenue variability (used to justify prudent guidance)
- Cloudflare OS extending AI tools to finance/legal/procurement
- **Not joining the AI capex race** (Scotiabank Q): "commodity compute is not an attractive model"; expects gross margin to stabilize ~73.1%, unit economics expand in 2H
- Q: securing AI agents — demand visible, agent-aware controls across SASE/Zero Trust

1. **Valuation:** ~180x forward EPS; ~42x sales; TradingKey pre-earnings flagged 48x sales at record high, "little room for error"; InvestingPro lists NET among "Most Overvalued"; stock above consensus PT
2. **Deceleration optics:** Q3 guide +31% vs Q2's +36% print; sell-side expects 26.5% growth over next 12 months vs 30.4% two-year (StockStory); Jefferies pre-earnings warned a modest raise could disappoint
3. **Bloody GAAP:** -$170M net loss; TTM GAAP loss -$206M; no GAAP profitability until 2028 target (though pacing ahead)
4. **Structural gross margin compression:** 73.1% vs 76.3% YoY (-320bps); compute-heavy developer mix (Finsee: "structural" concern, floor unknown)
5. **FCF margin whipsaw:** 8% in Q2 vs 13% in Q1; heavy capex for AI inference
6. **Insider selling:** ~$60M+ CEO/CFO/director into the pop
7. **Revenue variability risk:** pool-of-funds/consumption shift (Seifert's own caution)
8. **Execution risk:** 20% workforce cut (1,100+); President of Revenue **Mark Anderson retiring late 2026** (Morgan Stanley flagged GTM execution uncertainty)
9. **Competition:** hyperscalers and point solutions in edge/AI inference
