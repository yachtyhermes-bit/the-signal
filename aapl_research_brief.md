**Segment breakdown (record June quarter across the board):**
- **iPhone: $54.25B, +22% YoY** (vs $44.5B yr ago) — record June quarter, beat ~$53.5–53.9B est
- **Mac: $10.35B, +29% YoY** — crushed $8.74B est (memory-driven price hikes didn't dent demand)
- **Services: $30.74B, +12% YoY — MISSED ~$31.2–31.3B est.** Hit by App Store regulatory changes (EU DMA-style business-model changes in several markets) + mobile gaming softness. Still: **1.5B paid subscriptions**, ~75% gross margin, and July 20 price hikes (Apple Music, Apple One, AppleCare+) cushion growth.
- **iPad: $6.19B, -6% YoY — missed $6.92B est** (only blemish; June price hikes took effect in August)
- **Wearables/Home/Accessories: ~$7.89B, +6% YoY** (computed residual)
- **Greater China: $18.8B vs ~$19.5B est (Bloomberg) — MISS vs expectations**, though up ~28% YoY from ~$14.7B (sharp rebound; Cook: double-digit growth in *every* geographic segment)
- Operating cash flow $34.4B record; R&D record $11.73B; installed base all-time high across all categories; dividend $0.27 payable Aug 13 (record Aug 10).

**The beat was real — and the stock still crashed.** The market's problem wasn't the past quarter; it was the forward quarter and the memory-cost trajectory.

## 4. Q4 Guidance — the disappointment (Sept quarter, FY2026)

| Guide | Apple | Street |
|-------|-------|--------|
| **Revenue growth** | **9%–11% YoY** | ~12–12.1% consensus — **MISS** |
| **Gross margin** | **47%–48%** | ~48%+ expected; down from 50.1% reported |
| **iPhone revenue growth** | ~15% | 17.6% — **MISS** |

- CFO Kevan Parekh: supply-constraint impact will **"increase significantly"** in the September quarter; cited FX headwinds too.
- Tim Cook: **"very significant supply constraints"**; supply-chain flexibility is limited; Apple is **"evaluating all options"** for alternative component sources. Root cause, per Cook: *"we're having an incredibly strong product cycle beyond our expectations, and the [advanced chipmaking] supply chain just fundamentally has less flexibility in it to meet the high levels of demand."*
- Shortages of **advanced chipmaking capacity + memory** are limiting supply of iPhones, Macs, and some iPads. **Constraint is supply, not demand** — high iPhone demand will NOT be met by supply in the September quarter.

## 5. The Memory Shortage — the core story

- AI buildout has sucked up DRAM/NAND/HBM supply; **memory prices have roughly doubled over the past year**. Hyperscalers absorbed production capacity; consumer-electronics giants are now fighting for scraps (Seagate blowout, SK Hynix record quarter show the same squeeze).
- **Cook's "100-year flood":** Apple paid **more for memory in each of the past three quarters** and expects costs to rise again in the September quarter. Cook: *"If you look beyond September, we see the market pricing for memory continuing to increase, which could drive an increasing impact on our business."* He called the situation a **"100-year flood on memory pricing"** that won't recede anytime soon — and warned **more memory suppliers won't necessarily mean lower prices.**
- **Pass-through so far:** Apple **"reluctantly raised prices"** 17–25% on Macs and iPads in June (effective August) — that alone triggered the stock's worst session in a year at the time. **iPhones spared... for now.**
- **Jefferies (Edison Lee):** memory costs could cut iPhone gross margin from **38% → ~34.5%** in H2 2026; even with iPad-style price hikes, incremental gross margin would only be ~21%.
- **KeyBank (Brandon Nispel) — the bearish chain reaction:** iPhone price hikes → unit growth slows → user growth slows → **Services growth slows** (the multiple-compression nightmare for the bull thesis).

## 6. The Friday, July 31 Crash — by the numbers

- **Jul 30 (Thu) close: $333.43** (fell ~3% after-hours, then slid further as the call's guidance sank in).
- **Jul 31 (Fri):** opened **-8.6%**, traded down as much as **~9.3%** (low ~$303.10), closed **$308.91, -7.35%** on 132.3M shares (~2x normal volume).
- **~$360B of market value wiped** ($4.90T → $4.54T; Reuters pegged it at **$361.6B**; at the -9.3% intraday low, MarketWatch computed up to **$460B**).
- **Worst post-earnings day since Jan 24, 2013** (-12.4%, the first-ever iPhone miss); worst single-day drop since **March 16, 2020** (-12.9%) — Dow Jones Market Data via MarketWatch.
- Worst opening move since **August 2024** (Berkshire halved its stake).
- The Nasdaq still finished **+1%** — Amazon's +15% surge (record market-cap gain) offset Apple; the "AI capex boom" narrative diverged sharply between cloud winners and hardware-cost losers.
- Apple **fell back behind Nvidia** as the world's most valuable company.

## 7. iPhone 18 Cycle — the September overhang

- **September event:** iPhone 18 Pro / Pro Max (+ first foldable "iPhone Fold", possibly iPhone Ultra) launching with the redesigned, Google-Gemini-powered **Siri AI** — the centerpiece AI upgrade that's been the stock's 2026 driver (Apple pays Alphabet ~$1B/yr for Gemini vs the billions hyperscalers spend on capex).
- **Expected price hikes:** WSJ math says **iPhone 18 Pro starts at $1,299** (up ~$200 from iPhone 17 Pro's $1,099; some analysts flag up to **$300**); Pro Max reportedly $1,349–1,399. Analysts widely expect hikes "up to $300" on Pro models.
- **Mix shift, not volume:** reports (Forbes, MacRumors) say Apple is **delaying the entry-level iPhone 18 to spring 2027** — launching Pro models first this fall to defend ASPs and margins in the memory crunch. Pro models are also positioned as the exclusive gateway to the full Siri AI/Apple Intelligence suite.
- **Mitigants:** Apple Upgrade leasing program launched July 28 (Klarna-backed, iPhones to Macs); Siri AI stays free (though heavy users may be steered to paid iCloud+ tiers — a new Services monetization angle); $100B buyback authorization (April 2026) supports EPS.

## 9. Narrative Threads / Angles

1. **A beat that became a crash** — record June quarter, 16% growth, $2.02 EPS, and the stock's worst post-earnings day in 13 years: guidance + memory costs trumped everything.
2. **The "100-year flood"** — the AI buildout's collateral damage: the world's most valuable company can't get cheap memory, and it's passing the bill to consumers (17–25% Mac/iPad hikes already; iPhone 18 Pro $1,299 next).
3. **Supply-constrained, demand-rich** — rare position: Cook says demand is so strong supply can't keep up; the September revenue miss is a capacity problem, not a demand problem.
4. **Valuation reset** — from 38x trailing at Thursday's close to 35.5x after a $360B single-day haircut; still up ~14% YTD and ~53% off the 52-week low.
5. **Leadership transition shadow** — Cook's final call ends with a crash; John Ternus takes over Sept 1 with iPhone 18 pricing (and the memory war) landing in his lap.
6. **Bear case chain reaction** (KeyBank): iPhone price hikes → unit growth slows → user growth slows → Services growth slows — the bull thesis's biggest risk.
7. **China nuance** — Greater China +~28% YoY (rebound), yet still $700M under expectations: the market wanted a boom, got a boom-let.
